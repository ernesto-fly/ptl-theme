Parking

Complimentary parking is available in the field lot across from the venue, first-come, first-served. Attendants direct traffic on event day, ADA spaces are available, and there is roundabout access at the entrance for drop-off and pickup.

Rideshare

Uber and Lyft drop-off and pickup use the entrance roundabout. Rideshare is the easiest option if you are staying downtown or on the beach.
