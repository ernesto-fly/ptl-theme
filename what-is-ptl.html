What is PTL

The Premier Trading League is a full season built on Tradeify: two open trader leagues, a 16-creator division, and one $1.3M prize pool — all converging at a live LAN Grand Final at the Fort Lauderdale War Memorial on December 10, 2026.

Structure

Three tracks, one season

Two trader leagues run in sequence — PTL S1 in Sep–Oct, PTL S2 in Oct–Nov. The Creator Division runs in parallel across both, then all three converge at the finale.

Help

FAQ

Everything you need to know about the season and the finale. Still stuck? Reach us on the socials or the contact page.

What is the Premier Trading League?
PTL is a season-long trading and creator competition from Tradeify and FlyQuest. Two open trader leagues — PTL S1 and PTL S2 — run back-to-back while a 16-creator division (PTL-C) runs in parallel, all converging at a live LAN finale in Fort Lauderdale on December 10 for a $1.3M prize pool.
How do I enter?
Register free on Tradeify in August, then play PTL S1 (Sep–Oct), PTL S2 (Oct–Nov), or both. You can optionally join a creator's company along the way. Top 4 from each league earn a LAN seat. See the Play page for the full path.
How much does it cost to compete?
The two trader leagues are open and free to enter. Each match gives you a fresh $50K simulated account with no drawdown limit. An optional $29 tournament reset can wipe one bad match during M1–M4.
When and where is the finale?
December 10, 2026 at the Fort Lauderdale War Memorial (800 NE 8th St, Holiday Park), Fort Lauderdale, FL. It is a one-day live LAN Grand Final for the top 8 traders.
What is the prize pool?
$1.3M across the season: $400K at the LAN Grand Final, $300K in each trader league (PTL S1 and PTL S2), and $300K in the Creator Division. The LAN champion takes $180K.
How does a match work?
Each match is a contained head-to-head event. You're paired against another trader on a fresh $50K sim account and score points for winning the matchup (+3) plus profit points (+1 per $500, capped at +20). Highest closing P&L wins the matchup.
What is the Creator Division (PTL-C)?
16 invited creators each run a company as the CEO, paired with a pro-trader CMO who advises. Their audiences join as traders and compete under their banner across six game days. Top 8 companies reach the LAN, and every creator who plays at least 3 game days is paid.
How do I watch if I can't attend?
Every league and the live finale are broadcast on Twitch and YouTube. Live standings run on Tradeify throughout the season. See the Watch page.
How do I get tickets?
Finale tickets are sold via SeatGeek across four tiers, from General standing to invite-only VIP. See the Tickets page for what each tier includes.
Where should I park?
Complimentary parking is available in the field lot across from the venue, first-come, first-served, with attendants directing traffic on event day. See the Travel page for driving directions and rideshare info.
Is there an age requirement?
Age policy for the finale is being confirmed. Check back before tickets go on sale, or follow the socials for the announcement.
