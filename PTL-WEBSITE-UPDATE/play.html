Play the PTL

Traders enter through a simple flow: register free, optionally join a creator company, then play PTL S1, PTL S2, or both. Top 4 from each league earn a LAN seat. Company membership runs in parallel — it doesn't replace league play.

Register to LAN

A trader's path

01 Register — Create a Tradeify account and register for the PTL. Entry is open and uncapped. August · free · open
02 Join a company — Pick one creator's company for the season. Your trading feeds their standing. One pick per trader. Optional · PTL-C
03 Play PTL S1 — Eight head-to-head matches over four weeks. Survive the Match 4 cut; finish top 4 to reach the LAN. Sep–Oct · 8 matches
04 Play PTL S2 — A fresh field and a second shot at a LAN seat. S1 traders can re-enter. Oct–Nov · 8 matches
05 LAN Grand Final — The eight qualified traders play live for the $400K pool at the Fort Lauderdale War Memorial on Dec 10. Top 4 from each league

Trading

How a match works

Each match is a contained event. Traders are paired head-to-head on a fresh simulated account — no drawdown limits, no qualifiers. Highest closing P&L wins the matchup.
