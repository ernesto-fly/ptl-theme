Scoring

How you score

Step 1

The matchup

Pick one outcome per match.

WIN Win head-to-head — higher closing P&L than opponent. +3
DRAW Draw — within $50, both traders earn 1 pt. +1
LOSS Loss head-to-head — lower closing P&L, still earn profit pts. 0
Step 2

Profit points

Added on top of the matchup result.

+$500 Per $500 profit — capped at +20 per match. +1
−$500 Per $500 loss — uncapped, full downside. −1
Match score = matchup result + profit points
The top 4 by points in each of PTL S1 and PTL S2 compete for $400K cash and glory in Ft. Lauderdale.
