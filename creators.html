How you back a creator

It all happens on Tradeify. Pick one creator for the season and your trading powers their company up the standings.

01 Sign up — Create a free Tradeify account.
02 Pick a company — Join the PTL and choose your creator — one pick for the whole season.
03 Trade all season — Your trading feeds your company's standing on every game day.
04 Win together — Your creator's milestones unlock rewards that flow back to you.

How a company works

The creator, a pro, and you

Every company is a duo plus a community: the creator up front, a pro trader in their corner, and their audience competing under the banner.

01

The creator is CEO

02

A pro is CMO

A Tradeify pro trader advises your creator on strategy through the season.

03

You're the traders
